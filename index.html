Delete Your VarsityPlay Account

In accordance with our data retention policies and Google Play terms, you can submit a request to permanently delete your VarsityPlay account and associated data.

What gets deleted:
  • Login credentials and contact email
  • Player profile, rank records, and personal preferences
  • Assigned team manager or coach roles
Retention & Anonymization: Match results from completed tournaments will remain on public leaderboards, but your handle and identity will be permanently anonymized. Requests are processed within 30 days.
To complete your request:
  1. Click the button below to generate a pre-formatted email.
  2. Send the email from the address registered to your account.
  3. Include your In-Game Handle / Player ID.
